abstract: >
  We demonstrate a prototype differential atom interferometer based on the
  single-photon clock transition of fermionic 87Sr. The experiment validates
  common-mode laser phase-noise rejection at the standard quantum limit and
  demonstrates recovery of coherent oscillatory signals under strongly
  randomized phase conditions, providing an important step toward
  very-long-baseline atom interferometers for gravitational-wave detection
  and searches for ultralight dark matter.
